These three contiguous intervals use timestamps from root (0, 0) only. Other PEs can compute during the root’s completion interval. Consequently, the final segment is not a measurement of communication alone.

Evidence and limits

A passed case includes every registered session and control. A timed-out case can contribute only complete, correctness-checked repetition sets under the saved partial-timing policy; it still counts as a failed workflow. Changed and restored input sessions are retained in the raw observations; this explorer shows first unchanged-input sessions.

The matrices are small synthetic inputs with exact integer-valued checks. Full and bounded accumulators are manually constructed reference variants. These measurements establish neither physical WSE latency nor improvements produced by an API model.
