Sparse Products on the Mesh: SpMV, SpMM, SpGEMM

The dense pages followed MeshGEMM's tiles around a ring. Sparse products move differently: the matrix is a list of (row, column, value) triples, the work per PE follows the nonzero pattern, and what crosses the fabric is not the matrix but the dense operand. This page follows three reference kernels from the sparse study, traced in the simulator on small integer instances so that every product, sum, gathered value and packet can be checked by hand and, here, against the trace bit for bit. The same kernels run at benchmark size; those cycle counts are quoted from the study's records.

From a sparse matrix to PEs, wavelets and colours

Three storage decisions come before any dataflow: how the nonzeros are listed (CSR by row, CSC by column), how the list is cut across PEs, and what has to travel. The three kernels make three different choices.
