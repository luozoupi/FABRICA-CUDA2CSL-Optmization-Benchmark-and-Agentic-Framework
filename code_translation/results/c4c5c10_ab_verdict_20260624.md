## Is the GoL pass→fail a C10 regression? No — draw-noise.
Read both runs:
- **GoL-OFF**: passing W1 translation on attempt 1; all 12 steps were the W2 OPTIMIZER
  reducing cycles 6844→3147 (task_simplify / comptime_hoist / fmac_bulk).
- **GoL-ON**: never landed a passing W1 translation; thrashed in the REPAIR loop on
  bucket-B FABRIC-ROUTING stalls — "D2H 0 bytes received", "same-color send/recv
  collision across PEs", synchronous-@mov32 self-deadlock.

Those are GoL's documented deadlock-free-exchange ceiling (it historically only passed
*with* the deadlock hint, at attempt 5). C10 supplies reserved *id-range* facts, which
are orthogonal to deadlock routing. The arms differed only in which stochastic W1
attempt happened to land — the exact N=1 confound Finding-A warns about. GoL is a
fragile kernel (~3/11 pass-rate in prior multi-seed work).

## Verdict (Finding-A discipline)
**NOT PROVEN.** No ≥2-kernel margin; net-negative single draw; the lone delta is
fragile-kernel variance, not a fix effect. The three C4/C5-targeted fails held as fails
on both arms. Single-draw A/B cannot separate a real effect from translation variance —
same conclusion as the WS3/WS4 levers; a multi-seed cycle-delta eval is the proper test.

## Why these stay DEFAULT-ON anyway (unlike WS3/WS4)
WS3/WS4 were speculative optimization hints → kept default-OFF until proven. C1–C5/C10
are **corrections of wrong/missing platform facts** (illegal `@get_color(24)`,
hallucinated `DIM_LENGTH`/`dim_size`, untyped `output_queues = {}`). They are firewall-
safe (no benchmark answers), cannot make a correct translation wrong, and carry no
integrity/overfitting risk. They remain default-on as platform-fact corrections; the
honest headline records **no measured pass-rate lift at N=1**, not a proven lever. The
gate `XKERNEL_FIXES_C4_C5_C10` exists to re-run this A/B (ideally multi-seed) later.

## Caveat
N=1 per arm. Pass/fail is the only signal here; cycle deltas on the held kernels (e.g.
PM 43289→31438, BiCGSTAB 58495→32285) are W2-optimizer + simulator variance, NOT
attributable to C4/C5/C10. Sources: VERDICT.txt + per-kernel run.log under
